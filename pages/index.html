Get prepared

Every disaster is different. Here are suggestions for what to do first.

  1. Sign up for alerts

    Get local emergency alerts here and follow trusted sources.

  2. Prepare your home

    Get ready for potential heavy rain and wind. Clear gutters, fix roof leaks, and secure sandbags if necessary.

  3. Know your flood risk

    Find out whether your home or workplace is in a flood-prone area or downslope of an area recently affected by wildfire, where heavy rain can increase the risk of flooding, debris flows, and mudslides.

  4. Have an evacuation plan

    Know where you would go and how you would get there if you need to leave quickly. Keep an emergency kit ready, including plans, medications, and supplies for pets and other animals.

  5. Never drive through floodwater

    Water on flooded roads can be deeper and faster-moving than it appears, and roadways may be damaged or washed out beneath the water. Turn around and find another route.

  6. Stay informed

    Pay attention to weather forecasts and sign up for emergency alerts from your city or county. Conditions can change quickly during major storms, so follow instructions from local emergency officials.
